I'm Jia (Jiayang He, 何嘉阳), a designer and creative technologist in New York.

I studied jewellery design at Central Saint Martins and now study at ITP, NYU Tisch, where I work on physical computing, creative coding and data visualization.

Jewellery taught me to design for the body first. I still start there: rings that read finger movement, lace that works as a circuit, interfaces you wear rather than hold.
