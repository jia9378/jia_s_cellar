The lead

Ornaments that sense the body

I’m Jia. I trained as a jeweller, and now I make wearables, sensor systems and data stories at ITP, NYU Tisch.

By Jiayang He (何嘉阳) New York

Jewellery taught me to design for the body first. I studied it at Central Saint Martins, and I still start there: rings that read finger movement, lace that works as a circuit, interfaces you wear rather than hold.

At ITP I work on physical computing, creative coding and data visualization. The projects in this issue range from gloves that play notes and steer 3D models to a data story told through a particle brain.

Each one is printed here as a plate in its own palette and type, the way it was designed.

Abstract

Five works made between London and New York: two gloves, a data story, a solar rover and an AR cell. Each starts from the body or the senses and ends as working hardware or code.
